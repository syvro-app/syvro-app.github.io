The core idea

Your bank balance isn't your real answer.

A number that includes money you already owe to next week's bills isn't safe to spend — it just feels that way until the bill hits. Syvro fixes the math.

01

Add your bills once

Enter your bills and expenses by hand — no bank connection required. Set them as one-time, weekly, or bi-weekly, and Syvro handles the cadence math correctly, even across month boundaries.

02

Syvro reserves ahead of time

Before a bill is due, Syvro sets that money aside in your daily safe-to-spend number — so it stops looking like money you can spend on something else.

03

You see what's really free

What's left is your actual safe-to-spend reserve — updated daily, with Payday Intel giving you a forward look at how things stand until your next paycheck.

Built different, on purpose

Privacy & security, not as an afterthought

This is a deliberate product decision, not a missing feature.

No bank account linking. Ever. On any tier.

No Plaid, no MX, no OAuth to your bank, no screen-scraping. You type in your own numbers, always. That's not a v1 limitation — it's the design.

Nothing leaves your device

No backend, no cloud sync in v1.0, no analytics pipeline harvesting your financial data. Syvro behaves more like a notes app than a connected fintech product.

Encrypted at rest

Sensitive data is stored using EncryptedSharedPreferences, and app access is protected by your device's biometric prompt.

We disclose trade-offs, too

Where a deliberate security trade-off exists — like screenshot-blocking being temporarily off while store-listing screenshots are captured — we say so instead of leaving it quiet.

What "reserved" actually means

  • Reserving is a planning tool inside the app, not an actual funds lock at your bank. Syvro can't freeze money in your real account — only your bank can move real money.
  • Reserved funds release automatically at due date + 3 days, or the end of the calendar week (Sunday–Saturday) — whichever comes first — even if you never tap a button.
  • Only mark a bill as Posted after it's actually cleared your bank. Marking it early just makes your in-app number wrong — it doesn't touch your real balance.
  • Releasing funds in the app doesn't cancel or reverse a real-world payment. Syvro is a ledger you keep, not a system that talks to your bank.

We'd rather explain this clearly than let anyone assume Syvro does something it doesn't. Read the full Privacy Policy for the complete details.
